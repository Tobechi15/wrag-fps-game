The Loop

How It Works

Every match runs the same four-part cycle. There's no partial credit for surviving — the only thing that converts a run into something real is reaching extraction while you're still standing. Get killed first, and everything you earned that match is gone.

The Interface

Nothing Is Hidden

The HUD keeps the stakes visible at all times — your position, your current pot, and how exposed you are to nearby threats. Everything you've earned this match is marked "at risk" until the moment you extract. There's no ambiguity about where you stand.
