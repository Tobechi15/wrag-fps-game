House Ledger

Every number below is a real running total from the fake-points economy - see DEV_README.md's "House ledger" section for exactly what each one tracks. Never touched by Survival (unstaked) or the Deposit button (a demo top-up, not real revenue).

Fees Collected 0 PTS

The house rake actually withheld from kill payouts and Private stake-pool wins - the literal "fee" on every payout (HOUSE_RAKE_PERCENT, see .env.example).

Profit 0 PTS

Net stakes collected minus winnings paid out - the full P&L, including stakes a player never won back at all, not just the rake fee above.

Stakes Collected 0 PTS

Gross entry stakes ever deducted from a real balance (Versus/Coop queue-join, Private room create/join).

Stakes Refunded 0 PTS

Stakes refunded for leaving a queue/room before a match started - a neutral event, not a loss.

Net Stakes Collected 0 PTS

Stakes collected minus stakes refunded.

Winnings Paid 0 PTS

Real payouts credited back to players - match winnings and Private stake-pool shares.
